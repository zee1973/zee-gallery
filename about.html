About the channel

Zeewaqar Husnain is a YouTube channel built around cinematic AI-generated films, commercial-style AI ads, and stories from ancient history, alongside an original archive of hand-crafted 3D illustration work. New videos go up every week.

What you'll find here

  • Ancient Egypt stories — narrative videos covering figures like Hatshepsut and the Battle of Kadesh under Ramesses II.
  • Zee AI Commercials — cinematic, spec-style ads for fashion, sports, and luxury brands, made with AI video tools.
  • AI Dream Worlds — surreal, fantasy, and wildlife scenes built entirely with AI.
  • Samurai Micro Drama — a short-form AI-made historical series.
  • Holiest of the Holy Names — 3D calligraphy pieces honoring the names of the Prophet Muhammad's family.
  • 3D illustration archive — large-format (roughly 16,000 × 9,000 px) pieces of Quranic scripts, Egyptian hieroglyphs, and copper antiques, each revealed through a slow cinematic zoom.
